Delete your account

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Rumey account and the data linked to it. Rumey is operated by Abhishek Saini.

Option 1: delete it inside the app (fastest)

  • Open the Rumey app and sign in.
  • Tap Profile.
  • Tap Delete account (you can also go to Privacy & Data, then Delete my account).
  • Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: you cannot open the app

Send an email to nextgenai34f@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete your account and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, Google account identifier and the record that you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Story requests and shared stories sent from your device.
  • On your phone: your listening history, favourites and preferences are cleared when you delete the account in the app. Downloaded stories are removed when you uninstall the app.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Anonymous like counters on stories. They are not linked to you.
  • Reports you sent about stories. They are linked only to an anonymous device ID, not to your account.
  • Technical and security logs, for up to 90 days.
  • Copies in routine backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Limited information where the law requires us to keep it, or to resolve a dispute.

Your Google account

Deleting your Rumey account does not delete your Google account. To also remove Rumey’s access to your Google account, open your Google Account settings and remove Rumey under third-party access.

Questions

Email nextgenai34f@gmail.com. Our full Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.
