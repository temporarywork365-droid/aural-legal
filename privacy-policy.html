Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Sunoora collects, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you confirm that you are 18 or older and that you have read this Policy.

Summary

Here is the short version. The full details follow below.

  • Sunoora is an app for adults only (18+).
  • We collect only what we need to run the app: your sign-in details, your listening activity and your preferences.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not show third-party advertising.
  • Your listening history stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.
  • All stories are AI-generated works of fiction. Nothing in them is based on real people or events.
  • You can delete your account yourself, inside the app, at any time (Profile, then Delete account).

If you have any question about this Policy or your information, write to us at nextgenai34f@gmail.com or at Village Thakurpura, Block Saha, District Ambala, Haryana - 133203, India.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Sign-in with Google: your name, email address and Google account identifier (and your profile picture link, if Google provides it). We never receive your Google password.
  • Age confirmation: the date and time you confirmed that you are 18 or older. The date of birth you type on the age screen is used only on your device to work out your age. It is not stored and it is not sent to our servers.
  • Messages you send us when you contact support.
  • Reports you send about a story: which story, the reason you choose, and the anonymous device ID, so that one phone cannot send the same report many times.
  • Story requests and shared stories (only when the Requests feature is switched on): the text you send, an optional display name, and whether the post is private or public. A random, anonymous device ID is attached so we can apply daily limits and let people block authors.

Information created as you use the App

  • Listening activity: the stories you play, your progress, history, favourites and downloads. By default this is kept on your device.
  • Preferences: your mood and genre choices, filters and settings.
  • Likes: when you like a story, an anonymous counter on our servers goes up, and your device remembers which stories you liked.
  • Subscription status (when paid plans are available): which plan is active and for how long, provided by Google Play and our subscription provider. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

Technical information

  • App version, device model, operating system version and language.
  • Approximate region, worked out from your IP address.
  • Crash and error diagnostics, which help us fix problems.
  • A random, anonymous identifier created on your device to protect against abuse. It is not your advertising ID.
  • Like most online services, our infrastructure providers process your IP address to deliver the service and to protect it from attacks.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone input, text messages or call logs, and we do not collect your advertising ID. The App does not use cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account and keep it secure.
  • To run the App: play audio, remember where you stopped, show stories that match your preferences, and rank stories by likes.
  • To keep Sunoora for adults only, by recording that you confirmed your age.
  • To manage subscriptions and unlock paid features, such as early access.
  • To keep the service safe: prevent abuse, fraud and misuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To fix problems and improve the App, for example by diagnosing crashes and seeing which features are used.
  • To send you service messages and policy updates, and notifications only if you allow them.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (running the App), our legitimate interests (security, fixing and improving the App), your consent (where we ask for it, for example notifications) and legal obligations. In India, we process your data on the basis of your consent and for the legitimate uses permitted by law.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Supabase (sign-in, database and file storage), Google (Google Sign-In, and Google Play for distribution and billing), and RevenueCat (subscription management, when paid plans are enabled). They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal and safety: when the law requires it, or when needed to protect people, our rights or the security of the service.
  • Business changes: if we merge with or are acquired by another business, your information may be transferred, and we will tell you first.
  • At your direction: for example when you share a story using your phone’s share sheet.

5. Where your information is stored

Our database and files are hosted by Supabase in Ireland (EU West). Your information may be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including outside India, which may have different data protection laws. Where we transfer information internationally, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information: for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account inside the App, it is removed from our database immediately. Copies in routine backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Story requests sent from your device: removed from our servers when you delete your account.
  • Reports about stories: kept while we review them and for as long as needed to keep the App safe. They are linked only to an anonymous device ID, not to your account.
  • Listening history and favourites: on your device until you clear them or uninstall the App.
  • Anonymous like counters: kept without any link to you.
  • Technical and security logs: for a limited period, typically no longer than 90 days.
  • We may keep limited information for longer where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and enforce our rights.

7. How we protect it

We use encryption in transit, access controls, row-level security on our database and restricted staff access. We never store a password, because you sign in with Google. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you find a vulnerability, please tell us at nextgenai34f@gmail.com.

8. AI-generated content

Every story in Sunoora is a work of fiction created with the assistance of artificial intelligence, including its text and, where applicable, its voices and artwork. All characters, names, places, events and storylines are entirely imaginary. They are not inspired by, based on, or intended to depict any real person, living or dead, brand, organisation, community or real-world event. Any resemblance is purely coincidental.

The content is provided for entertainment only and is not advice of any kind. We show this notice with every story.

9. Story requests and content rules

When the Requests feature is available, you can send us a story idea or share a story you wrote. Every submission is checked automatically. We block explicit sexual content, hateful or violent content, anything that involves minors in a sexual context, instructions for self-harm or weapons, and links, phone numbers or social media IDs.

Private submissions can be seen only by the Sunoora team. Public posts are reviewed by us before other people can see them, and they show your display name (or “Anonymous”) and an anonymous code, never your email address or your real device ID. You can report a post or block an author inside the App. We may remove content, and stop accepting submissions from people who break these rules. Only send what you have the right to share.

13. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information and your account;
  • restrict or object to certain uses of your information;

How to use these rights: to delete your account, open Profile in the App and tap Delete account (it is also under Privacy & Data, then Delete my account). Your account is deleted immediately. Under Privacy & Data you can also clear your listening history. If you cannot use the App, you can ask for your account to be deleted by emailing nextgenai34f@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject “Delete my account”. For any other right, email us at the same address. We will reply within 30 days. You can also remove Sunoora’s access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings.

India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to access information about your data, to correct and erase it, to have your grievances addressed, and to nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom: you have the rights above under data protection law, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

California: you may ask to know, correct and delete your personal information. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we will not discriminate against you for using your rights.
